What the seasonal map says

Data. Nifty 50 (price index) month-close returns, Jan-2015 to Dec-2025, from public month-close records cross-checked against index levels. All figures are exact. Bank Nifty was deliberately left out of this full-year version — I couldn't source a clean, verified 12-month Bank Nifty history, and filling 130+ cells from memory would put unreliable numbers next to exact ones. The earlier Jul–Aug dashboard keeps Bank Nifty (flagged approximate). To add an exact Bank Nifty layer here, drop in a CSV of its monthly closes and it goes straight in.

Reading it safely. 11 years is a small sample; a single event (COVID Mar-2020 −23%, Apr-2020 +15%) can dominate a month's average — lean on the median and hit rate, not just the mean. Seasonality is context, not a signal. Not investment advice; past patterns don't predict future returns. Manage your own risk.
